# Use calibrated stopping only within intake safety and reviewer capacity

Test calibrated questioning as an intake aid with mandatory escalation. Do not monetize an unsafe benefits commitment away: its maximum rate is a separate feasibility constraint.

**Observed evidence:** Oracle household states share household-level dependence. The corrected certification API applies simultaneous binomial bounds over a fixed threshold grid and abstains when infeasible.

**Decision owner:** Benefits intake operations lead (proposed role, not a client).

**Base planning case:** Calibrated questioning and escalation. This is conditional on the assumptions, not deployment approval.

| Alternative | Economic cost | Cash outlay | Reviewer hours | Passes risk and capacity |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Specialist review of every intake | $156,000 | $0 | 2,400.0 | False |
| Calibrated questioning and escalation | $77,700 | $19,200 | 900.0 | True |
| Fixed short intake without calibration | $22,200 | $6,600 | 240.0 | False |

Economic cost includes valued staff capacity and scenario incident losses. Cash outlay excludes salaried capacity and contingent losses. These are not realized savings.

**Sensitivity across 27 equally enumerated scenarios:** Calibrated questioning and escalation: 9, Specialist review of every intake: 3, NO FEASIBLE OPTION: 15. Grid counts are not probabilities.

**Synthetic product experiment:** HOLD. Primary effect 5.0%; 95% interval [1.2%, 8.8%]. Guardrail effect 1.2%; interval [0.6%, 2.1%]. These numbers test the decision logic, not product performance.

**Priority order:**

1. Hold out entire households and freeze the stopping grid before calibration.
2. Budget escalation work and question burden together.
3. Choose a policy only when the safety gate and staffed queue capacity both hold.

**What would reverse the recommendation:** If no threshold is certifiable or escalations exceed capacity, the result is hold/increase staffing, not forced automation.

**Remaining uncertainty:** Oracle states are not observed applicant conversations. Zero monetized harm in this model means harm is governed by a hard gate, not that harm has no value.
