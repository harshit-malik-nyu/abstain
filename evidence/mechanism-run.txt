  It is not -- on THIS population. But the rule does not meet
  undetermined states uniformly; it commits on the high-scoring

              band  unsafe commits    flip  amount only

             above             208     208       0 ( 0%)
        well-above             385     308      77 (20%)

  72% for well-below, not 31%. The hypothesis was
  rejected on the wrong population, and is substantially right.
